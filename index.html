01 / ABOUT

Breaking attacks down.
Building detections back up.

I'm Sodik Tursunboev, a cybersecurity practitioner originally from Jizzakh, Uzbekistan and currently based in Warsaw, Poland. My work sits between offensive and defensive security: I emulate adversary techniques, inspect the telemetry they generate, build detection logic, investigate activity and validate whether defensive controls actually detect the behavior they were designed to stop.

My strongest technical direction is Detection Engineering and Security Operations, supported by hands-on work in Active Directory security, penetration testing, SIEM/SOAR development, purple teaming, threat hunting and security automation.

I have spent approximately three years independently studying cybersecurity and building practical environments, security tools and portfolio projects designed around complete attack-and-detection workflows.
